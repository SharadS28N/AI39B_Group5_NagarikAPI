The Story behind NagarikAPI

NagarikAPI was born from a simple observation: financial services in Nepal were being held back by paper-based identities and manual review. Customer onboarding took days, fraud was hard to catch, and legitimate users were dropping out of the funnel.

We realized that for Nepal's fintech scene to truly scale, it needed a "Source of Truth" — a single API that could verify any user, on any device, instantly.

Today, NagarikAPI is used by leading digital wallets, remittance companies, and commercial banks to verify thousands of users daily. We are more than just an OCR engine; we are the engine of trust in a digital-first nation.
